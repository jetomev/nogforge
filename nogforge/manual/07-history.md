# History

The **History** menu has two screens.

## Activity (5)

Every install, removal, update and clean-up nog ran, newest first, from nogForge or from a terminal, in plain words: when, what, who, and the result, with nog's own command underneath.

## nog Logs (6)

nog's own record, as nog wrote it (`~/.local/share/nog/logs/`): date, time, command, user, status and outcome, and underneath, which other logs nog wrote for that run. **Enter** (or a click) opens the run's full log in a window: for an update, every package with its versions, tier, nog's note and what happened to it (installed, held). nog writes a run's line when the run ends, and its package lines as they happen; the window shows the lines between the previous run and this one.
